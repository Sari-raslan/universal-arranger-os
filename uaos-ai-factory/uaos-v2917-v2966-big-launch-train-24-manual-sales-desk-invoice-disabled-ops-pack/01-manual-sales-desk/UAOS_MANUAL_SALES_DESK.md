# UAOS Manual Sales Desk

Status: MANUAL_SALES_DESK_READY

Purpose:
Prepare manual pre-sales operation without activating payment or issuing invoices.

Allowed:
- collect interest manually after owner approval
- prepare offer drafts
- track lead status
- reply with payment-disabled notice
- request more information
- keep customer delivery manual and blocked until owner approval

Blocked:
- real payment collection
- real invoice issuing
- automatic delivery
- public launch
- deploy
