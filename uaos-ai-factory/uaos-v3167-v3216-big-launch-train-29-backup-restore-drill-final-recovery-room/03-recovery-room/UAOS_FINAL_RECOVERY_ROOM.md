# UAOS Final Recovery Room

Status: FINAL_RECOVERY_ROOM_READY

Purpose:
Give the owner a safe place to understand what to do if the active local project breaks.

Default action:
Do nothing destructive.

If project breaks:
1. Stop work.
2. Do not deploy.
3. Do not payment activate.
4. Review backup evidence index.
5. Choose backup candidate manually.
6. Restore only into a new temporary folder first.
7. Compare files.
8. Owner approves before replacing anything.
