# UAOS Two-Lane Decision Explainer

Status: TWO_LANE_DECISION_EXPLAINER_READY

Lane A: Local Opening / Rehearsal
Meaning:
Run the whole launch experience locally. Open dashboards, pages, demos, customer preview, manual sales desk, and proof room.

Use when:
Now, before final legal/tax/payment/deploy approval.

Risk:
Low.

What stays blocked:
deploy, payment, public launch, invoices, automatic delivery.

Recommendation:
Use this lane by default.

Lane B: Public Activation
Meaning:
Publish to the public and/or activate payment and real customer operations.

Use when:
Only after owner has finalized legal/tax/business/payment/deploy data and gives explicit approval.

Risk:
High if done early.

What stays blocked now:
deploy, payment, public activation, real invoices, automatic delivery.

Recommendation:
Do not use now.
