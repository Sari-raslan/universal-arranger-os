# UAOS Internal Audio Player V15

Standalone local browser player for synthetic UAOS PC workstation preview.

Safety labels: PC_ONLY, UAOS_FORMAT, TEST_UNVERIFIED, SYNTHETIC_PREVIEW_ONLY, NOT_FOR_PA3X_LOAD, NOT_FOR_USB_TRANSFER, NOT_COMPATIBILITY_VERIFIED.

The player uses WebAudio oscillators only. It does not use samples, external audio files, copyrighted melody, or proprietary libraries. The MIDI link is preserved for DAW/Cubase preview.
