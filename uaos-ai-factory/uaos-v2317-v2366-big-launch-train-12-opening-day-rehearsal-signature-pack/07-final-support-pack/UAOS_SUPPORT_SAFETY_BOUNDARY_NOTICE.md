# UAOS Safety Boundary Notice

Status: SAFETY_BOUNDARY_NOTICE_READY

UAOS currently provides metadata, dashboards, documentation, and planning material only.
It does not provide keyboard binary output.
It does not provide SET/PCM/STY/KMP/KSF writer output.
It does not decode samples or parse PCM payloads.
It does not claim PA3X-ready output.
