# UAOS One-Hour Activation Runbook

Status: ONE_HOUR_ACTIVATION_RUNBOOK_READY_BUT_LOCKED

Use only if owner has received required documents and gives explicit approval.

Hour 0 checklist:
1. Fill business name.
2. Fill owner/company address.
3. Fill tax number / Steuernummer if available.
4. Confirm VAT/Kleinunternehmer decision.
5. Finalize Impressum placeholder.
6. Finalize Datenschutz placeholder.
7. Finalize AGB placeholder.
8. Finalize Widerruf placeholder.
9. Confirm prices.
10. Confirm product wording.
11. Confirm payment provider.
12. Confirm deploy target.
13. Re-run final QA.
14. Owner gives explicit deploy approval.
15. Owner gives explicit payment approval.

Locked actions:
This runbook does not deploy.
This runbook does not activate payment.
It only prepares the operating checklist.
