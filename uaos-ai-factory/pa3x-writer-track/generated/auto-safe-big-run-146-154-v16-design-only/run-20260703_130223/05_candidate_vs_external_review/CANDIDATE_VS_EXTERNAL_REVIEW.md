# Candidate vs External Review

| Path | Safety | Usefulness | Risk | Owner approval needed | Blocked actions | Deliverables | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Future local-only TEST_UNVERIFIED candidate | Medium | Can test local generation assumptions | Higher than reports because candidate artifact exists | Explicit owner approval text | USB, PA3X load, fixture modification, proprietary content, App.jsx, deploy/payment | One local-only TEST_UNVERIFIED generated candidate later | Use only if owner explicitly wants candidate |
| Reports-only external review | High | Helps independent technical review without new candidate risk | Lower | Owner instruction for reports-only pack | Candidate creation, USB/external copy, PA3X load | Evidence pack, reviewer questions, decision forms | Safer than candidate creation unless owner explicitly wants a candidate |
