# UAOS Dataset Policy

Allowed sources:

- Original UAOS material.
- User-owned material with consent.
- Properly licensed datasets.
- Public-domain music.
- Synthetic generated training data.
- Metadata and general musical principles.

Prohibited sources:

- Unauthorized commercial sample libraries.
- Unauthorized proprietary keyboard styles.
- Native Instruments or other commercial library content without license.
- Commercial songs copied into training datasets without rights.
- Any dataset represented as legally cleared without human review.
