Local-first Mission Control for AI agents, automations and software programs.

### What it does
- Coordinates coding agents you already install (Codex, Claude, Aider, Ollama — whatever is actually present)
- Tracks tasks, status, and evidence on your machine
- Stops restricted actions until you approve them once, for that scope
- Exports redacted diagnostics without shipping secrets

### What it is not
- Not Asana / ClickUp / Jira
- Not a replacement for your AI providers
- Not a cloud workspace that uploads your repos by default
